# soft clinical futurism

This is the visual direction for stead onboarding, recap, insights, and other reflective surfaces. It should feel like a beautiful health instrument: calm, precise, ambient, premium, and a little futuristic without feeling loud.

Main app and workout live surfaces can stay black and intense. First-run setup and reflection surfaces should use this guide.

## feeling

- Soft, clinical, ambient, premium, measured.
- Health-tech, but not dashboard-like.
- Calm and nonchalant, not motivational or gamified.
- The product should feel like it knows what matters and does not need to over-explain.

One-line rule: make it feel like a health metric surfaced by a beautiful instrument, not an app trying to explain itself.

## palette

Base:

- Warm off-white: `#f4f3ef`
- Soft gray: `#c9c9c6`
- Ink black: `#1d1d1f`
- Muted gray text: `#9b9a96`
- White glass: `rgba(255,255,255,0.62)`

Ambient accents:

- Health orange: `#ff7a1a`
- Soft coral: `#ee8a7c`
- Pale blue: `#c8d9e7`
- Mist lavender: `#d8d2df`
- Acid lime: `#dfff00`

Use accents sparingly. Orange, coral, blue, and lavender belong inside blurred ambient gradients. Acid lime is only for tiny tags or confirmation states.

## gradients

Gradients should feel diffused, organic, and behind glass.

Use:

- Radial gradients or blurred layered color fields.
- Warm center with cooler edges.
- Low contrast transitions and no sharp stops.

Avoid:

- SaaS-style linear gradients.
- Purple-blue startup gradients.
- Decorative blobs that look pasted on.
- Gradients on every surface.

## typography

Use San Francisco system font. Keep type light, airy, and editorial.

- Hero number: `64-96pt`, dotted, mono, or digital when useful.
- Hero label: `16-20pt`, regular or light italic.
- Section title: `30-42pt`, regular/medium.
- Body: `15-18pt`, muted gray.
- Metadata: `13-15pt`, soft gray.

Copy is sentence case, mostly lowercase, and short. Do not use exclamation points or hype.

Good copy examples:

- `today's signal`
- `7,124`
- `2,876 steps left`
- `next move`
- `walk · ten min`
- `sun is out`

## surfaces

Hero surfaces:

- Large rounded glass panel.
- Radius: `28-40pt`.
- One central metric or one central decision.
- Soft ambient background.
- Tiny top label.
- Interpretation below the metric.
- Optional ruler, ticks, or quiet progress scale near bottom.

Cards:

- White or translucent white.
- Generous padding.
- Very soft shadows.
- No borders unless almost invisible.
- Cards should feel physical, not boxed-in.

Layout:

- Hero first, then a partial reveal of what comes next.
- One primary object per screen.
- Use negative space as a feature.
- Do not fill reflective screens with controls.

## motion

Motion should make the surface feel alive and expensive.

- Ambient gradient breathing: `6-12s` loop.
- Card entrance: opacity plus `8-16px` lift.
- Press feedback: scale to `0.98`, immediate haptic where possible.
- Metric changes: gentle count or crossfade.
- Screen changes: crossfade plus slight parallax.

Avoid:

- Bounce-heavy celebrations.
- Confetti.
- Busy loaders.
- Long blocking animations.
- Animation that makes repeated actions slower.

## stead usage

Use this style for:

- onboarding
- setup
- daily recap
- insights
- calibration screens

Use the black live style for:

- home action surface when locked in
- workout session
- rest timer
- voice logging
- urgent nudges

The product can have two modes:

- `reflect mode`: warm off-white, glassy, ambient, premium
- `live mode`: black, minimal, intense

## avoid

- Generic wellness SaaS.
- Heavy dashboards.
- Overusing acid lime.
- Explaining features in long paragraphs.
- Making every screen a gradient.
- Gamified streaks and achievement language.
